# 🟣 Research & Experimental Projects

> Open-source experimental benchmarks, paper replications, and scientific empirical studies conducted by students and mentors of AIML Club OCT.

---

## 🎯 Academic Standards & Integrity

1. **Honest Representation:** Never represent preliminary student experiments or class projects as peer-reviewed publications unless they have been officially accepted by a recognized conference or journal.
2. **Reproducibility First:** All experimental code must include deterministic seed configurations, exact environment dependencies, and clear instructions to reproduce reported tables and charts.
3. **Transparent Failure Reporting:** Document ablation studies, edge-case failures, and negative results with equal scientific rigor as positive improvements.

---

## 📂 Active Research Areas

- **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT):** Empirical evaluation of LoRA vs. QLoRA on small open-weight language models for domain adaptation.
- **Edge Computer Vision:** Quantization and latency benchmarks on Raspberry Pi / edge compute devices for real-time safety inspection.
- **Fairness & Bias in Tabular Prediction:** Analyzing algorithmic bias across demographic subgroups in educational and financial prediction models.
